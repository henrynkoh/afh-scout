One Zillow link, three controlled gates

The app separates what should be automatic from what still needs a human, an inspector, or the authority having jurisdiction.

1

Filter the listing

Price, active status, single-family use, bedrooms, garage capacity, HOA, sewer risk, and county tax basis.

2

Prove the return

Cash flow, NPV, IRR, operational payback, five-year ROI, DSCR, breakeven rent, and a return-sensitive 1–10 score.

3

Control the conversion

Garage slab and header, egress, ceiling height, WSEC, smoke/CO, HVAC, utilities, parking, septic, zoning, WABO and DSHS.
