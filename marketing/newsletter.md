# Newsletter

**Subject:** I built a Zillow-to-AFH deal screen for Washington properties

A property can have positive monthly cash flow and still be a weak five-year investment. That is the problem AFH Scout is designed to catch.

The input is a Zillow link. The app reads the related listing facts, applies an AFH conversion buy box, and evaluates 25 combinations of purchase price and monthly lease. Each row reports cash flow, NPV, IRR, payback, ROI, DSCR, breakeven rent, and a 1-10 decision score.

The default project costs are $20,000 for six months of mortgage/tax/insurance/HOA, $60,000 for the garage conversion, and $10,000 for contingency. Mortgage rates refresh from Freddie Mac.

The report then gives two negotiation numbers: the minimum rent required at the asking price, and the maximum purchase price allowed at the target rent. A WABO/DSHS checklist keeps the remodel, septic, egress, energy, parking, zoning, and licensing assumptions from slipping through.

AFH Scout can also run as a daily NWMLS monitor and surface only new listings or price changes.

Open the app and documentation: https://henrynkoh.github.io/afh-scout/
