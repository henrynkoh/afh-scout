# Threads

I made a property filter for a very narrow question: should I buy this NWMLS home, convert the garage, and lease the finished house to an Adult Family Home operator?

Drop in a Zillow link. It runs 25 price/rent cases and returns cash flow, NPV, IRR, payback, ROI, a 1-10 score, the exact offer threshold, and the WABO/DSHS risks that can kill the conversion.

Open source: https://henrynkoh.github.io/afh-scout/
