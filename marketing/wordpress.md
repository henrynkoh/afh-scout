## From Zillow Link to AFH Investment Decision

AFH Scout is an open-source acquisition control system for Washington buyers evaluating a garage conversion or remodel for long-term lease to an Adult Family Home operator.

The app combines three gates. First, it filters NWMLS inventory by price, property type, bedroom count, garage evidence, HOA, sewer risk, and county. Second, it calculates twenty-five purchase-price and rent combinations. Third, it presents the inspection and licensing questions that must be resolved before the conversion budget is trusted.

The base model includes a $20,000 six-month carry, $60,000 remodel, $10,000 contingency, 25% down, current mortgage rates, vacancy, management, maintenance, taxes, insurance, appreciation, and selling costs. Outputs include cash flow, NPV, IRR, payback, ROI, DSCR, breakeven rent, and a weighted 1-10 score.

The score is intentionally conservative. If IRR misses the hurdle rate, the final score is reduced even when monthly cash flow looks attractive. The report also solves backward for the purchase price or rent required to reach the investment threshold.

The routine can monitor selected NWMLS areas each morning, report only new listings and price changes, and preserve every prior screen.
