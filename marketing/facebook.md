# Facebook

I built a free screening tool for buyers looking for a Washington house that can be converted for an Adult Family Home operation.

Paste a Zillow link. AFH Scout checks the NWMLS listing, price, bedrooms, garage evidence, HOA, sewer risk, and county taxes. Then it runs 25 price-and-rent combinations and ranks the top five by cash flow, NPV, IRR, payback, ROI, and a 1-10 investment score.

It also tells you the exact purchase price or monthly lease needed to make the deal work, plus the WABO and DSHS questions to close before spending remodel money.

The current model includes $20,000 holding cost, $60,000 remodel, and $10,000 contingency. Every assumption is editable.

Live app and source: https://henrynkoh.github.io/afh-scout/
